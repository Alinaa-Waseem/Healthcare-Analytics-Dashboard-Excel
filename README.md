# Healthcare Analytics Dashboard – Excel

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive Healthcare Analytics Dashboard developed in Microsoft Excel to analyze hospital operations, patient activity, treatment revenue, doctor workload, and billing performance.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, including data cleaning and ETL using Power Query, relational data modeling using Power Pivot, DAX measures, Pivot Table analysis, interactive visualizations, and business insights.

The dashboard is designed to help hospital management monitor financial and operational performance and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall healthcare and hospital performance.
- Monitor total revenue and billing performance.
- Analyze patient and appointment activity.
- Evaluate doctor workload.
- Compare revenue across treatment types.
- Analyze payment methods and payment status.
- Identify monthly appointment trends.
- Build an interactive dashboard for hospital management reporting.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Data Cleaning & ETL

Power Query was used to clean, transform, and prepare the raw datasets before analysis.

### Data Preparation Steps

- Imported multiple CSV datasets into Excel.
- Checked and corrected data types.
- Standardized date fields.
- Checked duplicate records and primary keys.
- Identified blank and error values.
- Created appointment year, month, and day fields.
- Merged Billing with Treatments to bring treatment information into the billing analysis.
- Expanded required columns after merging queries.
- Validated payment status values.
- Prepared clean datasets for data modeling and analysis.

## Data Modeling

Power Pivot was used to create a relational data model.

### Relationships

- Patients → Appointments
- Doctors → Appointments
- Appointments → Treatments
- Treatments → Billing
- Date Table → Appointments
- Date Table → Treatments
- Date Table → Billing

The model uses one-to-many relationships between dimension and transaction tables where applicable.

## Key Business Insights

1. **April recorded the highest appointment volume** with 25 appointments, representing 12.5% of total appointments.

2. **September recorded the lowest appointment volume** with 11 appointments, representing 5.5% of total appointments.

3. **Chemotherapy generated the highest treatment revenue** at 128,855.68, contributing approximately 23.4% of total revenue.

4. **ECG generated the lowest revenue** among the analyzed treatment types at 96,224.24.

5. **The payment success rate was 32%**, with only 64 out of 200 bills successfully paid.

6. **Pending bills represented 34.5% of total bills**, with 69 bills awaiting payment.

7. **Failed bills represented 33.5% of total bills**, with 67 unsuccessful transactions.

8. **Sarah Taylor handled the highest number of appointments**, with 29 appointments, representing 14.5% of total appointments.

9. **Credit Card payments generated the highest revenue** at 201,382.43, accounting for approximately 36.5% of total revenue.

10. **68% of bills were either pending or failed**, indicating a significant opportunity to improve payment collection and follow-up processes.

## Business Recommendations

### Improve Payment Collection
Investigate the causes of pending and failed payments and strengthen payment follow-up procedures to improve successful collections.

### Focus on High-Revenue Treatments
Ensure adequate staff, equipment, and service capacity for high-revenue treatments such as Chemotherapy and MRI.

### Address Low-Demand Periods
Investigate the reasons behind lower appointment volumes during September and consider targeted awareness campaigns, promotions, or appointment reminders.

### Monitor Doctor Workload
Regularly review appointment distribution across doctors to identify workload imbalances and improve scheduling efficiency.

### Optimize Payment Channels
Maintain reliable credit-card payment facilities while continuing to support other payment methods for patient convenience.

### Improve Resource Planning
Use monthly appointment trends to improve staffing and resource allocation during high- and low-demand periods.
